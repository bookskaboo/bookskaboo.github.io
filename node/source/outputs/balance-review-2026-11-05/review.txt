Five minutes measures producer work with continuous supply and free output.
Setup, upstream manufacturing, map travel and cable latency are additional.
Wired synthetic QA verifies five-minute repeated-component intervals, with a
few seconds of cold-cable latency. Upgrades and parallel factories accelerate
completion. This replaces the old shared-material anchor and +/-10% parity;
the different corners intentionally no longer have equal raw-material bills.

1. Wire: three Plates -> two, for Iron/Copper/Mythril Wire. Basic Core Iron
   falls 6 -> 4; Automata Copper 6 -> 4; Hydro Copper 15 -> 10. The production
   operations remain while recurring extraction/smelting demand decreases.
2. Optionally lower Cooled Gun's extra Iron Plates 2 -> 1. Together with Wire,
   Medium Bot costs fall from 34 Iron / 20 Copper / 27 Wood to 30 / 14 / 22.
   Water stays eight and Stone two, averaged over complete ingredient batches.
3. A Wire change also needs Efficient Circuit A/Motor Copper Plates 2 -> 1
   to retain their material advantage. Test Mythril/composite recipes and fixed
   Recycler returns before adopting. No such adjustments are implemented here.
4. Reassess Map Node tier transitions after recipe changes: with Hydro access,
   purchase 29 costs 240 Copper / 120 Iron and purchase 30 costs 496 / 320.
   The lab time rises only 75 -> 80 seconds; adding Hydro to the synchronized
   sets creates the material spike. Locked tiers are not charged. Avoid stacking
   a cheaper map curve onto cheaper recipes without assessing combined demand.
5. Retain current early unlock prices for now. The playtest reached Automata
   unlock at 4:53 and Hydraulic Assembly at 15:17; these are not first-item or
   steady-production timestamps. Wiring/guidance stalls need separate treatment.

RESOURCE PRESSURE
The easiest Dwarven settlement needs two Medium Bots/minute: 68 Iron, 40 Copper,
54 harvested Wood, 16 Water and four Stone/minute at standard recipes, before
replanting support. Its final assembler runs at 83.3% theoretical capacity.
The Wire/Gun proposal lowers this to 60 Iron / 28 Copper / 44 Wood per minute
without changing assembly speed or AP. Tier-five settlement upkeep is twice the
bot demand; inspect the actual settlement tier rather than assuming the easiest.

The 34 completed Map purchases cost 339 Simple, 294 Basic, 214 Automata and
84 Hydro Cores. Recipe expansion: 5,844 Copper / 3,200 Iron / 4,564 Wood /
298 Stone / 756 Water. Wire alone reduces the same purchases to 4,996 Copper /
2,612 Iron / 3,846 Wood. These are calculated bills for the recorded sequence,
excluding other research, buffers and rework, not minimum victory requirements
or measured gross extraction. Some exploration was avoidable operator overhead.

Recovery: 20 Motors cost 80 Iron / 120 Copper / 100 Wood to create a 1,000-unit
drill deposit. Mineral regeneration supplies two/minute, or four after its second
research, far below sustained bot-factory metal demand. Infinite Golem reserves
still need extraction and logistics. Recovery does not establish smooth pacing.
