Necessary research speedrun to the end of tier 6

Current independent core recipes · Encounter simulation. This page is the control without early preparation.

Five seconds places one node and its first connection. Additional connections take an assumed 2.5 seconds. The one-hour candidate finishes in 58m 30s, using 404 placed nodes and 606 connections, with 16m 25s of waiting. It is a separate candidate, not adopted general prices or a verified live-game speedrun.

Endpoint and search

Finish Endgame Theory and the planned tier-6 factory. Select only its paid prerequisite closure plus Assembler and Road for production/map logistics: 16 paid projects, no optional efficiency or convenience research. Useful core outputs are produced only up to the route's remaining requirements, with zero discretionary surplus. This route does not sustain maximum lab throughput.

Search: eleven target rates per tier, uniform-rate starts and three coordinate-descent passes, then a search over research-cost multipliers. This is a grid-local result, not proof of a globally optimal route. Reuse is counted for matching operations; more flexible machine repurposing or build ordering could improve it.

Limits of the model

  • Five-second node-plus-first-connection actions are the user's assumption; extra-connection cost, 50% support and 1.5 connections/node are modeling assumptions.
  • No planning, mistakes, travel, map adjacency, source-node placement, finite ore, camp suppression or explicit waste transport/stalls. Resource access and delivery uptime are assumed sufficient.
  • Production is a recipe-work capacity model with integer machines. Commissioning at 55% of placement and ramping production is an abstraction, not a simulated build order or recipe pipeline.
  • No optional upgrades are bought. Core yields, five-second Extractors and recipe costs are unchanged between the general and one-hour cases; only required research counts change.
  • The one-hour target is separate from the three-hour full-throughput checklist and the tier-7 20–30-hour endgame target.
