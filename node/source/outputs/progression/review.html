Implemented research progression

Simple, Basic, Automata, Hydro, Shiny and Complex Core production and research are active in the game. Tier 7 remains a design discussion. Cores share intermediates without consuming other cores.

Targets still require playtesting: roughly one hour for necessary-only speedrunning, three hours for maximum-throughput tier 6, and 20–30 hours for efficient full endgame. Minimum endgame coverage: 37–60 productive maps; maximum: about 109 of 121. These are design targets, not measured outcomes.

Expansion and future decisions

All 120 additional world cells would require Exploration and another 119 Map Node repeats, costing 3,136 simple-core, 3,091 basic-core, 3,010 automata-core, 2,880 hydro-core, 2,700 shiny-core, 2,470 command-core. Full-world unlocking is not required by the proposed endgame target.

The master plan in docs/master-plan covers tier 7, final victory, transport limits, geography, waste tuning and real playthrough validation. Power is removed pending a useful integration design; Tree Planters operate without it.

Calculation assumptions

  • Building nodes and cables currently has no item purchase cost. Raw totals are research consumption only, not building costs, buffered surplus, defense, land clearing, roads, or extra map points. Power is removed pending integration design.
  • All Extractors and Research Centers use a 5s base cycle, including newly placed and loaded nodes. Lab upgrades reduce the cycle to 4.5, 4, 3.5, 3, 2.5 and 2 seconds.
  • Baseline uses unupgraded cycle times, new Extractors, and no manual building or travel time. Efficiency upgrades and parallel machines shorten production.
  • Machine counts are analytical lower bounds; sources/deposits, logistics, renewable support, roads and the single research center are separate. Tree Planters operate without Power.
  • Current stages are analytical buckets, not enforced in the current research prerequisite graph.
  • Tiers 4–6 use the implemented independent core recipes; Final-Core research is excluded from this six-Core analysis; its corner acquisition and provisional Reclamation balance require separate validation. Endgame coverage target is 37–60 productive maps minimum and 109 at maximum, out of 121 total.
  • 20–30 hours targets efficient play; low-throughput factories may take substantially longer.
