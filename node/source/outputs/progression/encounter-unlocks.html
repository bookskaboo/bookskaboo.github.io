Encounter based machine unlocks

Encountering a world node grants its first processing tools. Core recipes and efficiency/capacity improvements remain researched. This is an adopted design direction, not live gameplay.

First entry into an activated node map containing the matching world node. No extra click or camera hunt. Locked or merely generated maps do not count.

Lake

Encounter grants:

  • Lake Intake
  • Hydro Processor
  • Coolant recipe
  • local fluid links

Research still required:

  • Hydrochemistry: Copper–Carbon Dispersion recipe
  • Hydraulic Assembly: Hydraulic Actuator recipe, Ceramic Electrode recipe, Hydro Core recipe

Build water collection and coolant routing while preceding-core research runs. Hydrochemistry no longer owns these machines or initial fluid links.

Mythril Deposit

Encounter grants:

  • Separation Furnace
  • Refined Mythril recipe
  • Slag Hopper

Research still required:

  • Ore Separation: Mythril Mesh recipe, Shiny Core recipe
  • Mineral Recovery: Slag washing, Recovered Brick, Recovered Iron

Prepare refining before Shiny Core access. Slag stays guaranteed; random waste still starts with tier-6 intermediaries.

Abandoned Fabricator — proposed landmark

Encounter grants:

  • Advanced Assembler
  • compatibility with already-known Motor and Iron Gear recipes

Research still required:

  • Hydraulic Assembly: Hydraulic Actuator recipe, Hydro Core recipe
  • Precision Control: Precision Controller recipe, Advanced Actuator recipe, Complex Core recipe

Introduce the machine with familiar recipes before researching advanced applications. The landmark cannot require its own output to reach.

Derelict Sorting Station — proposed landmark

Encounter grants:

  • Waste Sorter
  • mixed Waste Hopper
  • waste batch links

Research still required:

  • Precision Control: tier-6 waste-producing intermediaries, Fines settling, Flux stabilization, Crust milling

Scouting can prepare handling early; probabilistic waste production remains tier 6.

Fallback: Precision Control also grants these handling tools if the station has not been encountered.

World and save rules

  • Guarantee a reachable Lake and Abandoned Fabricator before Hydro production, and reachable Mythril before Shiny. Random alternatives do not replace these guarantees.
  • Required encounters must be accessible with preceding map access, roads and early combat. Never require the tools, cores or advanced bots the encounter unlocks.
  • Retain early discoveries permanently. Machine access is immediate; advanced recipes still check research prerequisites.
  • Identify encountered nodes on the map and show granted tools and remaining research. Do not reveal encounters from undiscovered generated maps.
  • Persist discovered node categories and granted unlocks. Older saves credit qualifying maps already visited.

Timing estimates

Run results with discovery time and early preparation. These separate conditional scenarios now schedule encounters; the earlier general/speedrun reports remain controls.

No core costs or research prerequisites are automatically removed. Existing simulations group construction by tier and assume resource access; they cannot measure early preparation or scouting delays. Their numbers remain the previous construction model until discovery and cross-tier building are explicitly scheduled.

Preserved decisions

  • Equal quantities of every core through the highest required tier
  • Single Home Research Center
  • Five-second Extractor base cycle
  • Tier-6 introduction of probabilistic waste
  • Paid escalating efficiency and convenience research
