Why this attempt halted
The S2 Iron Plate distribution could not be restored reliably across the whole Medium Bot chain after my scaling/merge change. Gear and Frame inputs starved while a Smelter held Plates. A direct Smelter-to-Gear test made the Gear Refiner and Sealed Frame assembler cycle; that temporary diagnostic was undone. This narrows the failure to the distribution path but does not establish an engine bug. Operator wiring assumptions and missed/ambiguous connections are a material confound. The added Merger/Joint route remains unverified. The Road route itself previously carried an actual bot to S5.

Progression guidance to review after the run
1. The research modal should identify the missing supplied Core, e.g. "Waiting for Automata Core". Identical 3/18 counters do not reveal which input is absent; I had to inspect the Home Research Center.
2. Mythril Refining asks for Tribute at a Dwarven settlement. The camp tutorial explains 60-second AP checks and shrink/growth, but the inspected UI did not explain which Dwarven locations qualify, how a settlement/Tribute is obtained, or the measurable completion condition. I pursued the S5 Camp and then explored a Village at S5.E2 without confirming the intended Tribute step.
3. Show bot AP values and received/required AP for the next check, plus the effect of a successful check. Current observations do not establish whether a camp-to-settlement conversion exists.
4. Show a concrete production-block reason such as "Waiting for Charcoal" and numerical buffer counts. Gray filled/empty pips and typed cable colors are easy to misread at map-fit zoom.
5. Road help clearly states direct Carts at both endpoints and 12 items/minute. Connections that cannot transfer should explain that locally. Typed/colored cables alone were repeatedly misleading as delivery proof.
6. Make depletion and the unlocked Mining Drill discoverable from the current stalled objective. The normal catalog says 20 Motors create a 1000-unit deposit; this option was found but not used.

Measurement and control limitations
This is not a clean speedrun or completion-cost estimate. Much of the elapsed 6:38:18 was operator setup, rewiring, diagnosis and waiting for incorrectly supplied chains. Undo was used as a normal gameplay control and was observed to restore the running/paused state and resource snapshot; gross extraction and cumulative consumption cannot be reconstructed from remaining deposits alone. Use the CSV observations as snapshots and the research spending file as confirmed project costs.
The user allowed artificial time acceleration for this test. No global speed control was found or applied; the test remained at 1x. In-game Dev/Mods overviews were inspected only for such a control; no grants, unlocks, exports, rule edits or mod application were used. Browser DevTools and game source were not used.
Browser controller calls containing longer action batches twice timed out at 30 seconds and reset the controller. ID-based rebinding recovered the same game without reload. Smaller action batches and a longer tool-call timeout worked afterward. This does not prove the cause of earlier connection problems.
