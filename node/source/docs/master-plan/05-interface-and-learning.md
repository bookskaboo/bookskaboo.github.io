# Interface learning and accessibility

## Current player tools

The interface includes Build, recipe selection and guide, Research and prerequisite navigation, Node Map, discovery journal, inspector, save/load, options and command help. The graph supports panning, zoom, selection, groups, recipe copying, grid alignment, shortcut bars and undo. Production uses motion, shapes and buffer indicators; rejected cable drops have visual feedback.

Research separates active work, guidance under Obligatory hand holding, available capabilities, future projects and completed projects. Future research stays browsable. Configurable machines require a recipe before connections; Basics explicitly teaches Copper Plate selection.

### Draft optional lesson: Mixed output and Filters

Author outline prepared for enjoyment criterion E05 (NODE-0807), under the existing SR-17 later-teaching scope. This lesson is **not implemented**. Its invitation, practice fixture, progression checks and player copy remain draft implementation inputs; no new campaign gates or lesson completion requirement are adopted here. Current mechanics are owned by [waste and recycling](../systems/waste-and-recycling.md) and [connections and routing](../systems/connections-and-routing.md), rather than older intended-behavior paragraphs elsewhere in this plan.

**Learning goal:** keep Controllers and Metallic Fines moving, identify a blocked Filter outlet and explain why storage is not disposal.

**Proposed availability:** offer optional practice when Precision Control and its Waste Handling discovery make the relevant recipe/tools available, before the player's first Controller batch where practical. Keep it replayable from Tutorials after eligibility. Declining or exiting cannot block production or research. This trigger is a draft to verify against real unlock timing, not implemented invitation behavior.

**Proposed presentation:** title **Mixed output and Filters**; supporting subtext **Production has acquired a filing problem.** Goal and action instructions stay literal. Reuse the existing practice card, shared reading-body/control roles, Restart/Exit/Finish behavior and independent practice engine; preserve the campaign and its prior pause state. The fixture may provide a working supply so the exercise teaches output diagnosis rather than rebuilding earlier tiers. Any supplied practice materials are lesson setup, not normal-play balance evidence.

| Stage | Draft player instruction | Practice action and evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Recognize the second product | “Each Controller batch produces 1 Controller and 1 Metallic Fines. Both leave through the same output stream.” | Inspect the Controller recipe/output and observe an actual completed batch. Check both resolved products, not only a progress bar or prefilled stock. Use ordinary recipe amounts and current family timing. |
| 2. Separate the stream | “Choose the material your Filter separates. Connect its selected-material outlet and its other-material outlet to valid destinations.” | The player chooses Controller or Metallic Fines, either selected outlet, node placement and cable layout. Require actual Controller delivery and Fines delivery to their matching destinations. Explain outlet identity using the actual control/socket labels when implemented; do not introduce new labels silently. |
| 3. Inspect the Waste Bin | “A Waste Bin stores waste. It does not destroy it. Check its stored Metallic Fines before and after another delivery.” | Observe actual Fines accumulation and the displayed finite capacity. Connecting a Bin is insufficient evidence. Do not wait for a full default 200-unit Bin just to teach storage. |
| 4. Diagnose the stop | “Disconnect one Filter outlet while material needs that path. Inspect the Filter and identify the blocked outlet.” | Let the player choose the branch. With the relevant material waiting, inspect the real blockage state and stop of new forwarding through the whole Filter. Already-dispatched cargo may still arrive. Ask which material/path is blocked before revealing the explanation. On request, explain: “A required outlet is blocked. The whole Filter waits until that path can accept material.” |
| 5. Restore flow | “Restore a valid route for the blocked material. Keep both outputs moving.” | Accept reconnection or another valid downstream route/storage arrangement; no fixed coordinates or preferred factory solution. Require the blockage to clear and actual new post-repair Controller and Fines dispatch/delivery after earlier in-flight cargo has settled. Inspect the source for available ingredients/output so unrelated starvation is not mistaken for a failed repair. |

**Independent finish check:** ask the player to explain why the useful product stopped, identify the affected outlet and show where the Fines are now stored. Completing the repair and recognizing stored waste are the learning evidence; a Continue click, cable existence or one old in-flight arrival is not. Let a player request a short factual explanation or restart without changing campaign progress. Leave recovery recipe construction and Reclamation as follow-up help; this short lesson does not require those chains or introduce Power.

**Checks required before calling it implemented:** unlock/invitation timing; skip/replay/exit and campaign isolation; both selected materials and selected outlet positions; blocked selected and remainder paths; full receiver and disconnected path; preservation of buffered/in-flight items; actual stop, repair and fresh delivery; unrelated input starvation; practice card focus, scrolling and narrow viewport; newcomer diagnosis after returning to ordinary play. The outline has source/factual and Markdown-link review only; none of these new lesson behaviors or human outcomes has been tested yet. Existing mixed-output/Filter implementation is not changed by the outline.

#### Shortage navigation implementation plan

Prepared for E06/SR-16 under NODE-0809. The user requested a plan, not implementation. The adopted NODE-0332 outcome above is retained. The following is an executable scope for later code work; every proposed module, callback, result type and new check remains unimplemented. Current Core badges still open recipes. These are proposed data fields, not new saved state. Pass current state/clock/catalog context explicitly; call from the user action, not every tick/render. Recompute and revalidate before applying a navigation result so load, undo or a completed project cannot use stale nodes.

**Shared facts, not a second simulation.** Reuse current [research eligibility/payment rules](../../src/simulation/researchStatus.ts) and [progress calculations](../../src/ui/researchProgress.ts), including frozen Map Point quotes and parked contributions. Inspect [production explanations](../../src/renderer/productionVisuals.ts), [routing](../../src/simulation/logisticsRoutes.ts), [material tracing](../../src/simulation/connectionMaterials.ts), [Filter routing](../../src/domain/filterRouting.ts), [Road materials](../../src/simulation/roadMaterials.ts) and [transfers](../../src/domain/types.ts) before implementing. Do not use the optimistic resource-recovery budget as proof of an actual connected shortage, and do not call helpers that reconcile/mutate queues merely to diagnose. No broad engine refactor is part of this plan.

**Resolver sequence:**

1. Read the actual active project and outstanding types. Separate paid research contributions from stock still in a lab and from stock elsewhere. If game/research processing is paused, or the relevant lab is disabled, processing-paused, absent or constructing, explain that known state in the fallback rather than claim an upstream manufacturing deficit. One lab must hold its own complete outstanding set; never sum partial sets across labs. Current unmodded placement allows one lab, but malformed/legacy multi-lab states need conservative handling.
2. Start at that lab's required Core inlet and walk upstream through its real, material-compatible connected graph. Follow actual configured recipe outputs and required inputs, storage/vehicle buffers, Joint/Splitter/Merger/Filter paths and direction-correct reciprocal Road shipments. Do not search every unlocked recipe or select an unrelated factory merely because it can make the material. At a missing input, follow its connected source where evidence permits; if it has no supply, the machine's own missing count is a concrete endpoint. An absent/disconnected Core producer uses the recipe/delivery fallback rather than an invented location.
3. Treat a paid/committed batch as work in progress, not as missing its already-consumed ingredients. Count only real compatible cargo on the relevant route, using amounts and endpoint/path identity; ignore cosmetic pulses, paid research Cores and unrelated sector/global stock. If available relevant stock/arrivals cover the next set/batch and no concrete blockage is known, use a delivery/processing fallback. Never infer rate insufficiency from a single empty-buffer snapshot or advertise a completion-time estimate without rate evidence.
4. Identify actionable local facts: a genuinely missing ingredient, unavailable connected reserve, a paused/disabled connected producer, confirmed obstruction or output/Filter blockage. A full source output is a delivery problem, not a missing raw material. For a required blocked Filter outlet, the causal branch may carry waste instead of the clicked Core; use the actual blocked outlet/cause and preserve all stock. Reuse current production/Filter status and ingredient counts in the target inspector. Do not equate theoretical Road capacity with measured delivery.
5. Resolve multiple paths conservatively. Follow an already unambiguous relevant route/context first. A healthy branch or sufficient incoming supply prevents treating another stalled branch as the sole explanation for lab waiting. If several producers/labs/branches yield materially different plausible causes, do not silently pick the first node by array order; return the recipe/delivery fallback. The initial scope has no new branch-ranking UI or claim to find the globally slowest bottleneck. Within a single clear chain, stop at its first supported actionable cause, not at a hypothetical ultimate resource bill.
6. Restrict traversal/targets to accessible, already discovered/materialized sectors under the existing discovery rules. Do not open/visit maps, spend points, generate layouts, reveal survey-only/unvisited contents or use saved landmark layout entries. For Unknown Device, real visible stock may be followed as cargo; do not infer or expose its hidden production formula. Respect fluids' local transport restrictions, actual Road pair direction/channel/material checks and active obstruction geometry. Current `roadTravelPair` depends on the active sector and validates travel rather than cargo direction; reuse/extract the appropriate pure pair validation instead of changing active sector during analysis.
7. Bound graph work with visited `(sector, node, material, port/direction)` keys and a documented node/edge work limit. Cycles, missing IDs, broken Road pairs, unsupported special flows and exhausted limits produce a safe fallback. Build temporary indexes per click; do not retain stale state references or create a new publication-time whole-world scan. Verify that supported representative chains fit the limit before choosing its final value.

**Navigation and return.** After revalidating a target, navigate using current sector/camera owners, retain the player's zoom within the existing 15%–220% bounds, center/highlight the target and select/open its existing inspector with missing available/required counts or the concrete blockage status. `App.tsx`'s normal `selectNodes` automatically opens configuration for recipe/filter nodes; use a deliberate inspect-target path so a shortage click does not unexpectedly open the recipe picker. Account for its sector-change effect resetting configuration/camera. Diagnostic computation must be read-only; the eventual click may change normal view/selection/navigation state but never production/research/stock. Do not pause/resume, rewire, edit recipes or checkpoint a gameplay command just to inspect.

Retain source Upgrades selection, filters/search, list scroll and badge focus for return. The current recipe fallback keeps ResearchPanel mounted but hidden; extend that focused origin handling or retain a small transient UI context for field inspection instead of storing navigation in saves. Closing/returning must respect topmost dialog ownership and existing Q/Escape conventions, without adding permanent expanded project details. Keep ordinary recipe access usable. Final return-control wording is implementation copy work; this plan does not silently relabel current controls. Announce the chosen material/cause through existing inspector/status semantics and make accessible action labels describe whether the click opens diagnosis or a recipe.
