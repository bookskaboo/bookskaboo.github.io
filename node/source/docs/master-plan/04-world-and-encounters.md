# World exploration and encounters

## Current geography

The world is an 11 × 11 grid, with Home at 5,5. Map tier is Manhattan distance from Home. Research funds Map Points and adjacent activation. The single Research Center stays at Home. Ordinary resource-bearing outposts offer Wood, Iron, Copper and Stone; Lakes and special encounters create additional reasons to specialize. Q14 changes intended Mythril access from deposits to Dwarven Tribute; this source transition is not yet implemented.

**Agreed:** branching production and alternative routes converge toward Home. This is a desired factory pattern, not an acyclic-graph restriction. Raw resources and intermediates remain valid solid cargo. Water-dependent work stays on a lake map because fluids cannot use Roads.

**Agreed discovery direction (Q13, November 2, 2026):** guarantee access to important discoveries within appropriate progression ranges, while varying their locations and layouts by seed. This includes Lakes, Mythril and progression landmarks. Reserve the four physical corner maps; Q77A shuffles which challenge occupies each one at new-game creation. Exact range bounds, resource counts, layout variation and accessibility checks remain to be designed and validated; this decision does not certify the current generator or change live generation.

## Expansion evidence

The completed Complex route used eleven active maps: Home, four productive outposts and six relay-only maps. This demonstrates a workable route and a substantial transit footprint in one layout. It does not establish an ideal ratio or universal expansion cost.
