# NODE map landmark proposal

[Editable proposal Page](https://chatgpt.com/space/page_17b0874940808191ab88e9a0b4ad953c) · [Master plan](README.md) · [Current map rules](../systems/map-nodes-and-exploration.md)

Proposal only. Selected direction and initial tuning for review; no runtime implementation. Selected direction recorded November 20, 2026 in NODE-0699 and NODE-0704. Keep this repository reference and the editable Page aligned when revising the design.

Permanent landmarks give players a reason to move production, build new supply routes, and explore another sector. Use 17 landmarks on the standard 121-sector world, including one additional Unknown Device. Their positions are chosen at world creation and revealed on a sector's first visit. Implemented locally for new standard 11×11 campaigns. The current runtime reference is `docs/systems/landmarks.md`; controlled headless verification and reports are in `outputs/landmark-implementation/`.

# Proposed landmark catalogue

The selected total is 17: keep all existing type counts and add one Unknown Device. The new landmark uses literal Core duplication with tier-8-only initial placement, arrival-order processing, five-Core input and output buffers and a two-space output requirement. Use the node title Unknown Device and show no recipes for it.

| Landmark | Count | Proposed effect | How it changes a factory |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Rapid machines | 6 | One fixed machine family; normal recipe inputs and outputs at 10% base cycle time. | Move several production lines toward one powerful processing site. |
| Tri Extractor | 6 | One ordinary extraction cycle creates three identical items from one reserve unit, with a separate buffer and cable outlet for each. | Build three branches around a deposit that lasts three times as long per delivered item. |
| Doubler | 3 | One each Refiner, Assembler and Fabricator. Ordinary inputs and family speed upgrades; double production. All normally unlocked family recipes except Cores, Yin and Yang. Double the entire resolved batch, including byproducts, waste and successful chance outputs; failed rolls stay at zero. | Concentrate scarce ingredients here, then rebuild downstream transport to handle the extra output. |
| Reclaimer | 1 | Perform unlocked Recycler recipes without Water, retaining ordinary non-Water ingredients and recovery quantities. Use four times base processing speed without stacking speed research. | Build a recovery hub away from a Lake and route waste there. |
| Unknown Device | 1 | Consume 1 Core of any tier; after a fixed 5 seconds, output 2 of that same Core. No speed upgrades. | Establish a dedicated Core production hub. |

# Operating rules

All landmark types are non-deletable. The existing Resource Relocation project will cover moving all landmark types while preserving their type, bonus, buffers and progress. Players may pause, disconnect and reconfigure recipes within each fixed machine family. Landmarks cannot be copied into templates, sold or converted into ordinary machines. Moving a Tri Extractor leaves its connected reserve in place unless the player explicitly selects both. When both are selected, move them together subject to valid group placement and connections. The Extractor still needs a permitted reserve connection after movement. The project becomes available only after recorded victory-research completion, retaining its current availability. Keep its one-time research bill of one of each of the seven Core types, seven Cores total. Landmarks may move only within their current Map Node; they cannot transfer between Map Nodes. After the one-time research purchase, eligible landmark moves have no additional material fee.

Landmarks are free to use once discovered. Recipe-based landmarks retain their normal machine and recipe unlocks and ordinary production ingredients. Unknown Device instead consumes an existing Core and duplicates it; the original Core manufacturing recipe, machine family and ingredient bill are not required. There is no activation or repair fee. Discovering a landmark does not unlock its recipes or bypass suppression, Lake access, corner encounters, research, or the existing shared Inventory gate. Ordinary factories remain sufficient to complete the game.

Each Rapid machine has a fixed family: two Rapid Refiners, two Rapid Smelters, one Rapid Assembler and one Rapid Fabricator across the six landmarks. One Rapid Refiner is the introductory landmark so it is useful early. Shuffle the remaining one Rapid Refiner, two Rapid Smelters, one Rapid Assembler and one Rapid Fabricator across the remaining Rapid slots by seed: two at tiers 3–4, one at 5–6 and two at 7–9. An advanced family may be found before it is unlocked and remain inactive. Recipe selection remains available within the assigned family.

Rapid machines always use 0.10 of base cycle time, giving 10× unupgraded processing speed. Ordinary family speed research does not stack with this bonus. Reclaimer always uses 0.25 of the ordinary base Recycler cycle time, giving 4× base processing speed. Normal Recycler speed research does not stack with this bonus. Tri Extractors use ordinary Extractor speed upgrades on their shared cycle. Each normal Doubler uses its fixed family's normal speed upgrades; the output bonus belongs to that node. Unknown Device uses a fixed 5-second cycle that research and speed bonuses cannot change. Production and recycling chains may reuse material produced by other landmarks.

Rapid machines, normal Doublers and Reclaimer keep ordinary machine buffer capacities unchanged, with no two-batch minimum or capacity bonus. Doubling a product does not double the Doubler's output-buffer capacity. A finished doubled batch larger than that capacity remains in paid batch state and releases its items in saved order as output space opens. No new cycle starts until all of that batch has entered the ordinary output buffer. The two eight-item Fabricator batches therefore remain usable without increasing capacity or excluding their recipes. Keep ordinary cable, vehicle and Road rates and their normal upgrades; landmarks grant no connection transfer-rate bonus. Faster processing is available capacity; actual delivery still depends on ingredients, buffers and transport. This is part of the incentive to redesign the line. Reclaimer has no Water inlet, Water buffer or Water debit; all non-Water inputs, research gates and output blocking still apply. Its ordinary Recycler counterpart continues to require Water.

The Tri Extractor starts with the ordinary 5-second base extraction cycle, shortened by ordinary Extractor speed research. It uses atomic batches: every completed cycle writes one item to each of its three buffers, each holding five items. If any buffer is full, the shared progress bar waits. An unused output eventually stops all three, so exploiting triple yield requires routing all three branches. Source access, guarded-deposit suppression, regeneration and natural depletion retain their ordinary rules. Triple yield also multiplies effective output from a replenishing reserve while stock is available.

Each normal Doubler can run every normally unlocked recipe in its fixed machine family except Core production, Yin and Yang. Brick, bot parts, mixed-product recipes and chance-output recipes are eligible. Material recovery loops are allowed: one Stone can become two Bricks in a Doubler and return as two Stone through recycling. This is intentional, rather than an eligibility error. There is no recipe whitelist or conservation-based exclusion.

Each Doubler keeps its fixed family and cannot convert between families. The icon and inspector identify the family while the node title remains Doubler. Keep one Refiner, one Assembler and one Fabricator, using ordinary recipe inputs, cycle times, speed upgrades, buffer capacities and transfer rules. Shuffle these families across their selected band slots by seed. Double every resolved output quantity, including secondary products and waste. Resolve each ordinary chance roll once, then double the resulting quantity: a failed roll still produces zero. Do not add rolls or change their probabilities.

## Unknown Device proposal

Add one Unknown Device as the 17th landmark. Its input accepts any Core: Simple, Basic, Automata, Hydro, Shiny, Complex or Final. Consume 1 supplied Core, process for exactly 5 seconds, then produce 2 of that same Core. This is duplication of an existing item, not a doubled manufacturing recipe: no original ingredient bill or machine-family recipe unlock is required. Cycle time is unchangeable; speed research and other speed bonuses do not apply. Process mixed Core inputs in arrival order: each cycle consumes the oldest waiting Core and produces two of that same tier. No recipe selector or player-selected tier priority is required. Keep a waiting-input buffer of 5 Cores total and a finished-output buffer of 5 Cores total, with each capacity shared across all seven tiers. The single Core committed to the active cycle is held separately and does not occupy either buffer. Before consuming the oldest waiting Core, require at least two free spaces in the finished-output buffer and reserve them for that cycle. If fewer than two are free, leave the Core queued and wait. On completion, place both identical Cores in the output buffer together; no separate completed-pair holding buffer is added. Preserve the six Rapid machines, six Tri Extractors, three normal Doublers and one Reclaimer. Place it in a seeded random eligible sector at Manhattan tier 8 only, subject to the existing placement and collision constraints. Show no recipe entry, recipe selector, ingredient bill or input/output recipe formula for Unknown Device. Leave its effect unexplained in player-facing text. Show only stored Cores, the active Core, progress and blocked status; retain the Unknown Device name in map details without revealing its function.

# Frequency and distribution

Count sectors, not the individual machine positions inside a sector. Home and the four corner sectors receive no landmarks, leaving 116 eligible sectors. Seventeen landmarks occupy 14.7% of those sectors, or about one in seven. Place at most one in any sector. Fixed totals avoid worlds with no useful discoveries or an accidental surplus.

Place the introductory Rapid Refiner in a seeded random eligible sector directly adjacent to Home. Exclude sectors containing monster camps or other interior obstructions, including Deep Holes, Lakes, living Golems and ruins. The existing clear E1 sector supplies a fallback candidate without moving existing encounters. Choose uniformly among the eligible first-ring candidates. Reveal its direction through the early exploration hint; show its type and bonus only on visiting. The guarantee is a reachable, signposted discovery within one sector purchase, while the player retains the choice of where to explore. Use a Tri Extractor for the other tier 1–2 slot, randomized among eligible sectors with a usable ordinary reserve.

| Manhattan tier from Home | Sectors in band | Landmarks | Selected type allocation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1–2 | 12 | 2 | 1 introductory Rapid Refiner; 1 Tri Extractor |
| 3–4 | 28 | 5 | 2 Rapid machines; 2 Tri Extractors; 1 Doubler |
| 5–6 | 40 | 5 | 1 Rapid machine; 2 Tri Extractors; 1 Doubler; 1 Reclaimer |
| 7–9 | 36 | 5 | 2 Rapid machines; 1 Tri Extractor; 1 Doubler; 1 Unknown Device restricted to tier 8 |
| Home and tier-10 corners | 5 | 0 | Preserve starting and final challenge sectors |
| Total | 121 | 17 | Shuffle remaining Rapid and Doubler families across their slots by seed |

These bands specify density, not independent roll chances. The denominator includes sectors that individual landmark types cannot occupy. Randomize among eligible sectors within each band; restrict the Unknown Device to Manhattan tier 8 only. Mix terrain and resource advantages rather than stacking every bonus on Rocky sectors. Do not change terrain quotas, Lake locations, existing ruins, settlement quotas, Golems, border obstructions or corner assignments to make room for a landmark.

After placing the introductory machine, distribute the other sixteen as evenly as the band quotas and eligible sources allow among the four quadrants. Assign center-axis sectors to a quadrant for counting only. The selected standard-world target is roughly three to five landmarks per quadrant overall, minimizing quadrant-count differences while allowing eligible sources and valid placement to take priority. Prefer sectors that do not share an edge with another landmark sector. This is a selected spacing preference rather than a hard prohibition; the early discovery, eligible sources and valid placement may require exceptions.

Tri Extractors need a usable reserve: select one of ordinary Wood, Stone, Copper or Iron, varying targets by seed. Players may reconnect the landmark to another eligible ordinary Wood, Stone, Copper or Iron reserve within the same Map Node, including after its first source is depleted. Reconnection does not grant movement before Resource Relocation is researched. Never add natural Mythril. Guarded and Golem reserves are excluded for both initial placement and reconnection in this initial version. Switching resource type discards the old stock in all three output buffers, matching ordinary Extractors. Reconnecting to the same resource type retains matching stock; switching does not require empty buffers. Reclaimer needs no Lake or Water supply and can occupy an otherwise eligible sector without either. Keep landmark footprints separate from all existing obstructions.

# Placement and discovery

Choose the complete landmark assignment with an independent seeded random stream after the existing world layout is assigned. Fix the introductory family as Refiner, keeping its location seeded, then independently shuffle the remaining Rapid family multiset and the three Doubler families across their respective slots before final footprint validation. Save a landmark layout version, each sector and position, type, machine family and linked reserve when applicable. The same seed and version must reproduce the same assignment; exploration order, reloads and research must not reroll it.

Reserve each footprint, sockets and usable connection approaches alongside existing generation reservations. Keep landmarks clear of Lakes, active Deep Holes, maximum settlement growth, Golem footprints, border art and required Road access. Linked Extractors sit beside their reserve rather than overlapping it. A landmark must fit in the initial field without Area Expansion or a late crossing upgrade.

Use one Landmarks filter for all discovered landmark types, with each landmark identified in the selected sector's Points of Interest. Do not add separate filters per type. Reveal a landmark only after its sector's first visit; purchasing or activating an unvisited sector does not reveal it. Hide its presence, type, exact position and bonus beforehand, with no anonymous frontier signals. Keep the early exploration hint that gives a direction without revealing the machine or bonus. Use pink for Unknown Device and purple for other landmark markers, paired with their names or icons. Unknown Device's Points of Interest entry gives its name without an effect explanation. Final marker art and help text remain implementation work.

Only new standard 11×11 campaigns receive the first landmark layout version. Existing saves keep their current fields and generate their unexplored sectors without landmarks; loading, importing or re-saving does not opt them in. Training maps keep their authored layouts. Existing-save conversion is outside this version; any later migration would require a separate design.

# Supported world size

The first landmark version supports only the standard 11×11 world, with the selected 17-landmark total. Larger worlds do not receive this landmark layout version. Larger-map landmark support and proportional count scaling are deferred; the previously proposed scaling formula is not part of this version. Existing supported larger-world gameplay is unchanged.

# Current system context

The current world is an 11 × 11 Node Map. Map tiers use Manhattan distance from Home, and all unlocked sectors keep simulating. E1 is reserved clear and E2 contains a guaranteed Lake. Existing family efficiency upgrades shorten cycles without reducing ingredients; normal Extractors have a five-second base cycle and five-item output buffer.
