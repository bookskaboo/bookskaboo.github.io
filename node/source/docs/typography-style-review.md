### How the hierarchy should work

Use sentence case for labels and instructions. Keep abbreviations and identifiers such as AP, map IDs, and key combinations in their established form. In dense views, wrap labels or give the row more room before shrinking text. Use tabular digits where changing counts need to stay aligned. Modal text now shares 4 px label gaps, 8 px paragraph gaps, and 16 px section gaps. Checked the Build, Research, Options, and welcome text in the local browser. Stored Suppression Bots share one icon and name with an AP suffix. A Filter lists the material once. Checked all 25 assemblies in the simulation. The Recycler matches its feed automatically. Browser checks confirmed the active recipe appears first and shows Waiting for Water when dry. Disposal shows No output.

Give essential text a clear foreground on its actual surface. Keep instructions and blocked states readable in dimmed views. Use a text label with color for meaning, and let a filled button distinguish primary actions. Technical strings use Cascadia Mono, Consolas, ui-monospace, then monospace.
