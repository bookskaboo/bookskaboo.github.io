Mix and playback proposal

  • Use recorded piano throughout, distinguishing direct actions, progress and warnings by register, rhythm and chord shape. Keep frequent cues shorter and quieter than rare milestones.
  • Play only after a successful committed change. Never sound on a ghost preview, a canceled dialog, a failed save, or a state restored from a save.
  • For future integration: mute plus master/effects volume, saved preferences, playback unlocked by user interaction, no catch-up queue after a hidden tab, and no repeating alarms. Pausing stops future simulation cues; its own confirmation can finish.
  • Proposed priority: victory / character upgrade → new important warning → ordinary research / discovery → direct action → optional navigation. A character upgrade replaces its research chime; a discovery replaces its travel cue. Keep at most four voices and one milestone at a time.
  • Essential information remains visible in notices, controls and status text. Ambience and music remain separate work within the previously agreed audio scope.
